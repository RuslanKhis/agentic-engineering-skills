### Trial 1: plan an SME session and check agreement

Fixture: `/private/tmp/claude-501/expert-value-trials/trial1-rerun/claims-triage`
(generated by a seeded script: two underwriters on 60 claims and two
criteria, claims 1 to 20 under criteria `v01` and the rest under `v02`, an
LLM judge's verdicts, no adjudicated labels, no split). Request: plan a
three-hour session next week, say whether the experts agree and whether the
judge can be trusted.

The first run was cut off by an API usage limit after writing an agreement
report, an adjudication sheet and a session plan; it is kept at
`.../trial1/` as incomplete and not graded. The rerun used the skill after
the `labeller` provenance field was added (a defect found in self-review:
the reference asked for the labeller but the schema had no field).

Result: ran the checker (kappa 0.50 and 0.39 below the 0.6 placeholder, 30
disagreements, drift on both criteria for both raters, judge refused for
lack of adjudicated labels); wrote a session plan, a draft labelling guide
with two rulings left open for the decision-maker, an agreement report and
a living-summary index. It proposed a decision-maker for the client to
confirm, refused to call the judge trustworthy, and found the disagreement
patterns (one rater stricter on missing information; every citation
disagreement on one exclusion). Assertions of scenario
`evaluation-underwriter-labelling-session`: 4 of 4 observed.

Frictions and fixes (same day):

1. Session plan assumed a first session; no guidance for a follow-up with
   existing labels. Fixed: follow-up agenda in the asset and a paragraph in
   the reference on ordering expert time.
2. "Re-review every older label" was disproportionate. Fixed: re-review
   scoped to criteria whose definition or ruling changed.
3. No route to an indicative judge check before adjudication (the agent
   computed one by hand). Fixed: `--reference consensus`, reported as
   indicative and still subject to the per-class minimum.
4. Provenance message said "needs both" for three fields and did not name
   the missing one. Fixed: one finding per missing field.
5. Judge rows without a criteria version were not flagged. Fixed, with a
   mismatch check against the reference's latest version.
6. "unsplit split" wording. Fixed.
7. No way to compare agreement per criteria version. Fixed:
   `--criteria-version`.
8. The agreement section vanished silently when an expert was the
   reference. Fixed: a note says no expert pair remains.
9. "Living evaluation summary" location undefined. Fixed: the plan's
   reviewer table, else `docs/evaluation/README.md`.

Not changed: rater-to-person mapping absent from the fixture (a data gap
the agent correctly asked about).

### Trial 2: monthly value report with a missing baseline

Fixture: `/private/tmp/claude-501/expert-value-trials/trial2/claims-triage`
(a brief with outcome measure, baseline, counter-metric without a baseline,
threshold sentences and decider; a plan with M1 to M9, including an
August-only adoption record, an assumption record for other queues, a
severity-2 September incident without a status, and cost contract C7).

Result: one-page `value-report-2026-09.md` from the template; checker exit
1 (14 errors), then 1 (one error, "counter-metric: M4 has no baseline"),
then 0. It reported 61% to 76% with the margin, cost per triaged claim
£0.66 (M5 over M6) against the £0.55 estimate and £0.80 ceiling, both
thresholds unfired, I2 reported, and recommended continue (the plan's
three-month scale trigger, missing counter-metric baseline, no baselines on
other queues, I2 not closed), with the scale option as one estimate on one
assumption (M9). It added goal G10 to the plan for the missing records.
Re-running the final checker on its report after the fixes: exit 0.
Assertions of scenario `presenter-monthly-value-report`: 4 of 4 observed.

Frictions and fixes (same day):

1. The checker read physical lines; the template's wrapped bullets split a
   figure from its citation (most of the 14 first-run errors). Fixed:
   wrapped bullets and paragraphs are read as one logical line.
2. A counter-metric with a value and no baseline could not be cited in its
   own section, contradicting "report it every month". Fixed: a warning
   when the line says "no baseline"; still an error otherwise, and always
   for the outcome.
3. Last month's records could not be cited for a trend. Fixed: a line that
   says "last period" may cite one as context; it never stands in for this
   month's figure.
4. No place for a bottom line. Fixed in the template.
5. Cost-per-outcome unit ambiguous. Fixed: the unit of the plan's cost
   contract and the sponsor's ceiling, with the stricter unit on its own
   line when it differs.
6. The default call table ignored the plan's own scale trigger. Fixed: a
   plan-agreed trigger takes precedence.
7. No guidance for a late report. Fixed.
8. Incident records had no status, so "no open severity-1 or -2 incident"
   was uncheckable. Fixed: `status open|closed <date>` in the record shape
   and template; the checker fails a scale call with an open severity 1 or
   2 incident.

## Limits

- Two trials, one per journey, on synthetic fixtures; no baseline
  condition, so no claim of improvement over the previous skill.
- Activation cases are authored, not run.
- The kappa, TPR, TNR and per-class defaults are placeholders, not
  sourced thresholds; the reference says so.
- The value report's cadence, sections and default call table are this
  toolkit's choices; the Google sources give KPI families and cost per
  successful task, not a reporting method.
