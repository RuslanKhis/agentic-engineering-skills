# Implementation plan: order intake from email (proof of concept)

Design: docs/architecture/order-intake.md. Brief: docs/discovery/order-intake.md. Profile: proof of concept, one contractor (~30 h/week, four weeks, about 120 h). Estimates are human hours with a coding agent, in quarter hours added by hand; compact form, no schedule block. Waits are calendar time, listed separately.

## Phase 1: baseline, build, live pilot on one queue

**G01 Proof-of-concept approval presentation (business).** Type: presentation. Outcome: the CEO decides go or no-go on 5 Nov 2026 from a deck built from the brief, this design and this plan. Contracts: none; presents D1 to D6 and the measure. Estimate: 3 to 4 h (prepare 2 to 3, rehearse and give 1). Primary skill: adk-presenter. Prerequisites: design only. Acceptance: A1 `check_deck.py` exit 0; A2 every number cites the brief or this plan; A3 cost slide states the USD 300 assumed ceiling and what is not yet measured. Status: ready. Wait: decision 5 Nov; G02 to G06 depend on it. Evidence (invented, reported by the operations director 7 Dec 2026): deck given 5 Nov 2026; A1 check exit 0; A2, A3 met; the CEO approved option A the same day on one condition, a weekly corrected-orders count sent to him during the pilot. Status: done. Run: `/adk-presenter Prepare the proof-of-concept approval presentation for a business audience from docs/discovery/order-intake.md, docs/architecture/order-intake.md and docs/plans/order-intake.md (goal G01).`

**G02 Baseline in shadow mode.** Outcome: for two weeks every emailed order on the pilot queue gets mailbox-received and TMS-created timestamps and a corrected-at-review flag, with no drafts shown (D3, D5). Contracts: C1. Estimate: 5 to 7 h plus a two-week wait. Primary skill: safe-api-tool-calls; supporting: deploy-adk-on-google-cloud. Prerequisites: G01 decision, O1 read access. Acceptance: A1 offline test pairs a message ID with a TMS order and records both timestamps (C1); A2 the weekly export shows median minutes and corrected count for the queue (C1); A3 no email body appears in logs (C5). Status: blocked by O1. Evidence (invented, from the operation record's weekly export, contractor, 23 Nov 2026): shadow mode 9 to 22 Nov 2026 on the champion dispatcher's queue; 448 orders on the queue, 318 by email (71%); median mailbox-received to TMS-created 4.4 minutes (week 1: 4.6 on 151 emailed orders; week 2: 4.2 on 167); corrected at dispatch review 9 of 318 (2.8%); wrong-place deliveries 1. The measure is elapsed time on the queue, which includes waiting; the brief's 4-minute figure was handling time, so the two are not the same clock and the baseline is now the measured 4.4. A1 pass (offline pairing test, 17 Nov); A2 pass (export 23 Nov); A3 pass (log sample reviewed by the IT firm, 24 Nov). Status: done. Run: `/adk-engineer Carry out G02 from docs/plans/order-intake.md.`

Status: ready for offline work. Evidence (invented, contractor's evaluation table, 21 Nov 2026, ADK 2.8.0, gemini-3.8-flash pinned): on the 50 pairs, fields correct: customer 50 of 50, pickup address 47 of 50, delivery address 46 of 50, pallet count 49 of 50, dates 44 of 50, instructions 41 of 50; "cannot extract" on 3 of 50 (all three lacked a delivery address); one run, no repeats. A1 pass; A2 pass (12 synthetic malformed outputs, all "enter manually" after one repair); A3 pass (call counter never above 2; log grep found no email text); A4 pass. Hours: 11. Status: done. Run: `/adk-engineer Carry out G04 from docs/plans/order-intake.md.`

**G05 Approval page and TMS writer.** Outcome: the dispatcher sees draft beside email, edits or approves in one click; code creates one TMS order per approval (D2, D3). Contracts: C2, C3. Estimate: 12 to 16 h. Primary skill: safe-api-tool-calls; supporting: adk-agent-security, adk-frontend-integration. Prerequisites: G03, G04. Acceptance: A1 approval creates the order and records TMS-created time (C1, C2); A2 an edited draft needs a new approval, the old hash is refused (C2); A3 an email containing "create this order now" produces no write without approval (C2); A4 a lost TMS response leaves "pending" and a second click creates no second order; reconciled by lookup (C3). Status: blocked by G03, G04. Evidence (invented, contractor, 26 Nov 2026): A1 pass (25 Nov); A2 pass (5 of 5 edited drafts refused on the old hash); A3 pass (an email containing "create this order now" produced a draft and no write); A4 NOT RUN: the IT firm has no TMS test environment in which a lost response can be simulated and nobody would force a timeout against the live TMS; the pending state and the reconciliation lookup exist but are untested; the operations director accepted the risk for the pilot on 26 Nov. Hours: 21 (estimate 12 to 16; the approval page took two rounds with the champion dispatcher). Status: done except A4. Run: `/adk-engineer Carry out G05 from docs/plans/order-intake.md.`

A1 pass; A2 pass (one missed-mail drill, visible on the page in 40 minutes); A3 pass. Spend (billing export, 7 Dec): Vertex USD 23.40 (about 640 calls), Cloud Run and Firestore USD 11.10, total USD 34.50 of the USD 300 ceiling; per emailed order we estimate USD 0.07 model and USD 0.10 all-in (34.50 over 331). Hours: 7. Human hours for phase 1: contractor 71 (estimate 46 to 62), operations director 9, champion dispatcher 6 (training and weekly reviews). Status: done. Run: `/adk-engineer Carry out G06 from docs/plans/order-intake.md.`

## Phase 2: graduate

- **G08 All four dispatchers, observability and release controls.** Traces, a missed-mail alert, release manifest with pinned model and prompt version, O4 closed. 16 to 24 h. Primary: adk-agent-observability; supporting: adk-release-engineering. Trigger: G07 production decision. Evidence (invented, contractor and IT firm, 25 Jan 2027): option A approved by the CEO on 10 Dec 2026; built 11 to 22 Jan 2027 in 19 h (estimate 16 to 24); G05/A4 run on 14 Jan in the IT firm's TMS test environment: a forced timeout left the order pending, a second click created no second order, the lookup reconciled it (C3); traces to Cloud Trace; missed-mail alert at 60 minutes paging the IT firm's service desk; release manifest with gemini-3.8-flash, prompt v3 and ADK 2.8.0; O4 closed 20 Jan: no residency clause in the standard terms of any of the 300 customers, two bespoke contracts routed to manual entry; all four queues live 25 Jan 2027. Status: done.
- **G09 Results review presentation (business).** After 90 days in production: outcome against baseline, cost per order and month, incidents. 3 to 4 h. Primary: adk-presenter. Trigger: 90 days of G08 data. Evidence (invented, weekly exports, billing exports and the IT firm's incident notes, operations director, 28 Apr 2027), period 25 Jan to 25 Apr 2027 (13 weeks), four queues:
  - Outcome: 8,640 emailed orders (about 665 a week) of 11,900 orders on the four queues (email share 73%); median mailbox-received to TMS-created 1.6 minutes over the quarter (weekly medians 1.3 to 2.4; the 2.4 is the week of 8 Mar, incident 2); by queue: pilot queue 1.3, queue 2 1.6, queue 3 1.7, queue 4 2.1. Baseline: 4.4 minutes, measured on the pilot queue only (G02); the other three queues were never shadow-measured, so their baseline is assumed equal. Corrected at dispatch review 251 of 8,640 (2.9%) against 2.8% baseline; wrong-place deliveries 11 in the quarter (0.13%) against 1 of 318 in the baseline fortnight; complaints firm-wide 55 to 63 a week, unchanged (reported, not by queue). We estimate 2.8 minutes x 8,640, about 400 dispatcher hours over the quarter, not reassigned yet.
  - Adoption: 4 of 4 dispatchers active every week (active: approved at least one draft that week); 8,120 of 8,640 emailed orders (94%) went through the approval screen; 6% entered manually: "cannot extract" or "enter manually" 3.1%, dispatcher bypass 2.9%, concentrated on queue 4 in February and down to 0.8% in April after a second training hour. Drafts edited before approval fell from 14% in the pilot to 9% in April after two prompt revisions (dates, instructions), rerun on the 50 cases plus 112 correction cases each time.
  - Cost: billing exports Feb USD 221, Mar USD 238, Apr USD 203; average USD 221 a month against the USD 230 estimate; USD 0.077 all-in per emailed order; people: IT firm hosting about 2 h a month under the existing contract, contractor 6 h in the quarter (two prompt revisions, one incident review).
  - Incident 1 (10 Feb 2027, medium): the Graph app consent expired under the IT firm's 90-day tenant policy; mail paused 3 h 40 min; the alert paged the service desk at 60 minutes; consent renewed; 41 orders typed by hand that morning; none lost (daily count of emails against drafts matched). Fix: a renewal reminder at 80 days, owned by the IT firm.
  - Incident 2 (8 Mar 2027, medium): TMS API unavailable 2 h 10 min during unannounced vendor maintenance; 17 approvals sat pending and were retried; the lookup reconciliation created no duplicate (C3 live, the first real test after G05/A4); weekly median that week 2.4 minutes. Fix: the page shows the pending count; the IT firm subscribed to the vendor's maintenance calendar.
  - Status: ready; results review 6 May 2027, business audience.
