## Rehearsal

Two full runs out loud, the second before a colleague playing the CEO who interrupts at the hours overrun; notes cover about 4.1 of 15 minutes (business) and 3.5 of 30 (technical), so the rest is the recorded demo, the one real order and questions. Question protocol: let it finish, restate, answer in one sentence, evidence with method and sample, bridge to option A; unknowns are "not measured" with the goal that measures them.
