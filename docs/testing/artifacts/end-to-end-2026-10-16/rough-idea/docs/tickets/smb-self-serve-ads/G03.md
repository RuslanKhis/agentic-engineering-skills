# G03 Price and availability from the services

## Outcome
After ranking, code attaches price and availability from the pricing and inventory APIs; the model never sees or states a price (C2, D1). Catalogue, pricing and inventory outages fall back without a retry storm.

## Acceptance
- [ ] A1 pricing service down: product shown with "price on the next page", logged (C2)
- [ ] A2 catalogue or inventory rejects or times out: today's page plus a sales link, one bounded attempt (C2)
- [ ] A3 the output schema has no price field; a price in a reason is rejected (C2)

Verification: offline tests with stubbed services.
