1. **Give production evidence an ID scheme and a template.** Acceptance cases have `G04/A2`; the quarter's outcome, cost, adoption, SLIs and incidents have nothing, so final-results and case-study citations degrade to "phase 2 evidence, outcome row". Add to the plan (via the designer's plan template, with a pointer at S:43-45) an evidence record per measurement, `M1`, `M2`, with value, baseline, window, sample, method, owner and build, and have the checker's `CITATION` regex accept `M\d+` and `I\d+` (incidents). The presenter's "every number cites its evidence line" becomes checkable instead of aspirational.
2. **Sync the documents with the checker and stop the filename collision.** `deck-format.md:80-83, 91-94` against `check_deck.py:60-63` (technical budgets 30/80, notes share 0.4 to 0.8); `stage-outlines.md:92` "or none" against the required `ask` tag for `final-results`; the hype regex matching a literal `always` in code spans; and `decision-memo.md` / `pre-wiring.md` named per stage (S:132-135, `deck-format.md:110-112`), since one topic has four decisions.
