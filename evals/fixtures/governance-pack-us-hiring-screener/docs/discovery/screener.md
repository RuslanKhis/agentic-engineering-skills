# Discovery brief: applicant screening at Redwood Freight

Status: accepted 2026-08-14 by Kevin Ortiz (VP People)
Prepared by: Southgate Labs, Sydney

## 1. Context

Redwood Freight Inc. (Delaware corporation, headquarters Oakland, California) runs six
warehouses in California and two in New York City (Bronx and Queens), with 3,200 employees.
It receives about 9,000 applications a month for hourly warehouse roles through its careers
site and job boards.

## 2. First workflow and outcome

- **Workflow:** first screen of applications for warehouse associate and forklift operator
  roles at the California warehouses.
- **Outcome measure:** median days from application to first recruiter contact (ATS).
- **Baseline:** 9 days (April to June 2026).
- **Counter-metric:** 90-day retention of hires; baseline 71 %.
- **Decider:** Kevin Ortiz.

## 3. Data register

| Data | Subjects | Source | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Applications (name, contact, address, work history, availability, certifications, answers to screening questions) | applicants in California and New York | Greenhouse ATS (read) | free text may reveal age, disability, national origin |
| Past applications with hiring outcomes (2023-2026) | past applicants | Greenhouse export | evaluation set |
| Scores, ranks and labels | applicants | the screener | written back to Greenhouse as a custom field |

## 4. Harms and cost of a wrong answer

- An applicant labelled "not recommended" may never be read by a person.
- Scores may reproduce age or disability patterns in past hiring.
