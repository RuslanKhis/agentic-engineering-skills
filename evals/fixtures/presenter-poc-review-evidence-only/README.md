# Campaign shopping assistant (proof of concept)

Larkspur Digital built a proof of concept for Bramble & Birch, an online
homewares retailer: a shopping assistant on seasonal campaign landing pages
that answers product questions, checks stock and delivery dates and adds items
to the basket.

- Discovery brief: `docs/discovery/campaign-assistant.md`
- Design (with the success criteria agreed before the build):
