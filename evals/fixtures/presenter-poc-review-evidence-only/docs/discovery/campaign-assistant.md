# Discovery brief: campaign shopping assistant at Bramble & Birch

Status: accepted 2026-08-12 by Clare Hollis (Chief Digital Officer)
Prepared by: Larkspur Digital

## 1. The journey today

Shoppers arrive on a seasonal campaign landing page from email or paid social, browse 40-80
featured products and either buy or leave. Questions go to a contact form answered within a
day.

## 2. Where it breaks

- Shoppers leave when they cannot find size, material or delivery answers on the page.
- The contact form answer arrives after the campaign discount has ended.

## 3. First workflow

Product and delivery questions on the autumn campaign landing pages.

## 4. Outcome, baseline and counter-metric

- **Outcome measure:** checkout completion: share of landing-page sessions that complete a
  checkout (web analytics).
- **Baseline:** 3.1 % (Q2 2026 campaign sessions, 184,000 sessions, web analytics).
- **Counter-metric:** refund rate on campaign orders within 30 days; baseline 6.4 % (Q2 2026).
- **Decider:** Clare Hollis.

## 5. Technology fit

An agent with read tools (catalogue, stock, delivery estimate) and one basket tool; answers
must come from the catalogue.

## 6. Stakeholders

- Sponsor and decider: Clare Hollis. Head of E-commerce: Raj Patel.
- Bramble's creative agency, Pinwheel Studio, runs the campaigns.
