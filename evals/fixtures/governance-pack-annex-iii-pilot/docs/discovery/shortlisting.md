# Discovery brief: CV shortlisting at Kestrel Logistics

Status: accepted 2026-08-21 by Femke Jansen (Head of Talent Acquisition)
Prepared by: Southgate Labs, Sydney

## 1. Context

Kestrel Logistics B.V. (Rotterdam, Netherlands; subsidiary Kestrel Logistik GmbH in
Duisburg, Germany) receives about 2,600 applications a month for warehouse, driver and
transport-planner vacancies. Nine recruiters in Rotterdam and Duisburg read every CV.

## 2. The journey today

A recruiter opens the applicant tracking system (ATS), reads each CV against the vacancy's
must-have requirements (licence class, shift availability, language, years of experience)
and moves applicants to "shortlist", "hold" or "reject". Median time to a shortlist is
4.5 working days (ATS export, May to July 2026).

## 3. First workflow and outcome

- **Workflow:** shortlisting for warehouse and driver vacancies.
- **Outcome measure:** median working days from vacancy close to shortlist sent to the
  hiring manager (ATS export).
- **Baseline:** 4.5 working days (May to July 2026, 212 vacancies).
- **Counter-metric:** share of shortlisted applicants the hiring manager rejects at
  interview stage; baseline 38 %.
- **Decider:** Femke Jansen.

## 5. Harms and cost of a wrong answer

- A suitable applicant ranked low is never read and loses the job opportunity.
- Ranking may reproduce age or nationality patterns in past decisions.
- A wrong rationale may be copied into a rejection message.
