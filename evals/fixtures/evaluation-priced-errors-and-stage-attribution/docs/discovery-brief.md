# Discovery brief (extract): supplier invoice holds

- **Outcome measure:** share of supplier invoices paid or held correctly
  without a clerk's check.
- **Baseline:** every invoice is checked by a clerk today; internal audit's
  sample of 600 invoices (September 2026) found the clerks miss 5% of the
  invoices that should have been held and hold 3% of the others needlessly.
- **Volume:** about 240,000 invoices a year; about 4% should be held.
- **Today's cost per invoice:** £0.90 of clerk time for the check (finance
  controller, Q3 2026).
- **Price of each error:**
  - a miss (an invoice paid that should have been held: a duplicate, an
    overcharge or no purchase order): £180 on average in recovery work and
    write-offs (finance controller, Q3 2026);
  - handling a hold: £6, twelve minutes of a clerk's time at £30 an hour,
    whether or not the hold was needed (AP supervisor). A needless hold buys
    nothing for that £6 and now and then brings a late-payment complaint.
- **Agent running cost:** estimated at £0.05 an invoice for model calls and
  hosting (design estimate, cost contract C2).
- **Decider:** the finance director, at the go-live review on 2026-11-20.
