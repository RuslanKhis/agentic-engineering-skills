# Householder Design SPD 2022 (extract, text only)

## Table 2 Minimum off-street parking (the table did not survive extraction cleanly)
1 bedroom: 1 space (Zone A: 0). 2-3 bedrooms: 2 spaces. 4+ bedrooms: 3 spaces.
Note 2: within 400 m of a frequent bus stop the standard reduces by one space.

## Section 4 Side extensions
Two-storey side extensions keep a 1.0 m gap to the side boundary where a terracing effect would
otherwise result. Visibility splays of 2.4 m by 43 m are required where a new or widened access
meets a classified road.
