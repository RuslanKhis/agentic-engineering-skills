# Brookfield Local Plan 2021 (extract, text only)

## Policy H3 Residential extensions
Extensions to dwellings are supported where they respect the scale, form and materials of the host
building and the character of the street. Rear extensions should not exceed 4.0 m in depth for
terraced houses or 6.0 m for detached houses unless no material loss of daylight or outlook to a
neighbouring habitable room results.

## Policy H4 Dormers and roof alterations
Dormers on front roof slopes are resisted in streets where none exist. Rear dormers are supported
where set back at least 0.2 m from the eaves and below the ridge.

## Policy T2 Parking
Proposals that remove an off-street parking space must meet the standards of Table 2 of the
Householder Design SPD, unless the site is in Zone A (town centre).

## Policy HE1 Conservation areas
Development in a conservation area must preserve or enhance its character; uPVC windows on front
elevations are not acceptable.

## Policy D5 Trees
Development must not harm trees protected by a Tree Preservation Order (TPO). Where works are within
the root protection area, a tree protection plan is required by condition.
