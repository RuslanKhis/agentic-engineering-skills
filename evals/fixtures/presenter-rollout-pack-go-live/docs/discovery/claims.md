# Discovery brief: claims triage at Fenwick Mutual

Status: accepted 2026-07-29 by Helen Okafor (Head of Claims Operations); people plan updated
2026-10-14 for the Dublin office

## 1. The journey today

A first notification of loss arrives by web form or email. A triage handler reads it, opens
the policy in the policy administration system, decides the claim type and urgency, notes the
cover position and routes the claim to a team.

## 2. Where it breaks

- Handlers re-read long policy wordings to find the clause that applies.
- Urgent claims wait in the same queue as routine ones.
- Notes are inconsistent, so the receiving team re-checks cover.

## 3. First workflow

Triage of new home claims.

## 4. Outcome, baseline and counter-metric

- **Outcome measure:** median triage time per claim (claims MI report), monthly.
- **Baseline:** 21 minutes (August 2026, 400 claims, Leeds).
- **Counter-metric:** re-routing rate after triage; baseline 9 %.
- **Threshold:** if the re-routing rate rises above 9 % in any month, the urgency field is
  paused until the cause is found.

## 5. Technology fit

An agent with tools drafting a recommendation for a person to accept; the handler decides.
