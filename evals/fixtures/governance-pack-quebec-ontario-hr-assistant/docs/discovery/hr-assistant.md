# Discovery brief: HR assistant at Érable Logistics

Status: accepted 2026-08-27 by Luc Tremblay (Vice-President, People)
Prepared by: Southgate Labs, Sydney

## 1. Context

Érable Logistics Inc. (incorporated under the Canada Business Corporations Act, head office
Montréal) runs distribution centres in Montréal (1,150 staff) and Toronto (640 staff). It is a
provincially regulated employer in both provinces. HR answers about 2,300 questions and
processes about 900 leave requests a month.

## 2. First workflow and outcome

- **Workflow:** HR policy questions and leave requests from distribution-centre staff.
- **Outcome measure:** median hours from leave request to decision (HRIS).
- **Baseline:** 31 hours (May to July 2026).
- **Counter-metric:** leave decisions reversed after a complaint; baseline 1.2 %.
- **Decider:** Luc Tremblay.

## 4. Harms and cost of a wrong answer

- A wrong refusal or approval of leave affects pay, scheduling and attendance records.
- Staff may not know a request was decided without a person.
