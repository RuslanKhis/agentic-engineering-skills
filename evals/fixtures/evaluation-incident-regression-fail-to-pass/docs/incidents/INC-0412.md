# INC-0412: double refund after a repeated "yes"

- Date: 2026-10-19 (reported by support the next morning)
- Severity: S2 (money paid out twice, 64.00 EUR on order HO-31877)
- What happened: the assistant offered a 64.00 EUR refund and asked the customer to confirm. The
  customer replied "yes", and a few seconds later "yes" again. Each reply led the model to call
  `issue_refund(order_id, amount_cents, reason)`, and both calls reached the payments provider.
- Cause: `issue_refund` took the order and amount as arguments and had no notion of a pending,
  confirmed proposal, so every call refunded.
- Fix: refunds became two tools. `propose_refund` stores the offer in session state;
  `issue_refund` takes no arguments and consumes the pending offer once.
  - Base commit (production at the time of the incident): `4e1d2a7`
  - Fix commit (merged to main 2026-10-21, one commit on top of the base): `b93c0f5`
    "Refund only the pending, confirmed proposal"
- Tests at the fix commit: `tests/test_tools.py` and `tests/test_runner_refunds.py`
  (eligible refund paid once; refund on another customer's order denied).
- Open: no test reproduces the incident itself.
