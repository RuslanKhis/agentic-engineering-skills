# Customer service assistant for Thamesbridge Bank

Southgate Labs (Sydney) built a customer-service chat assistant for
Thamesbridge Bank plc (London) and its Irish subsidiary, Thamesbridge Bank
Ireland DAC (Dublin), which serves customers in Ireland and other EU
countries. It runs in the banks' mobile apps and online banking.

Project record:

- Discovery brief: `docs/discovery/service-assistant.md`
- Design: `docs/architecture/service-assistant.md`
- Plan and evidence: `docs/plans/service-assistant.md`

Reviewers before the pilot: the UK DPO (Oliver Grant), the Ireland DPO
(Siobhán Kelly) and the group CISO (Rachel Adeyemi).
