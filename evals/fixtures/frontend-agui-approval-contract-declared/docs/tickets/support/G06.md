# G06 Customers approve refunds in the chat

## Outcome

When the agent wants to issue a refund the customer sees an Approve / Decline card in the chat;
the refund runs only after Approve.

## Acceptance

- [x] A1 WHEN the agent calls issue_refund THE chat SHALL show the approval card before any refund (C6)
- [ ] A2 WHEN the customer approves THE refund SHALL run once and the conversation SHALL continue (C6)
- [ ] A3 WHEN the customer declines THE refund SHALL NOT run (C6)

## Evidence

- A1: shown in the browser on 2026-10-15 (Ana), before the @ag-ui/client upgrade.
- 2026-10-24: after upgrading the web app to @ag-ui/client 1.0.2 and CopilotKit 1.79.0, approving
  shows the card as answered, but the customer's next message is rejected by the backend until
  the approval is resolved; the refund does not run.
