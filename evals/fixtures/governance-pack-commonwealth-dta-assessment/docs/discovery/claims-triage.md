# Discovery brief: claims triage at the Compensation Claims Office

Status: accepted 2026-09-02 by Helen Park (Assistant Secretary, Claims Operations)
Prepared by: Southgate Labs, Sydney

## 1. Context

The Compensation Claims Office (CCO) is a non-corporate Commonwealth entity under the PGPA
Act. 120 claims officers in Canberra and Adelaide triage about 1,900 new claims a month.

## 2. First workflow and outcome

- **Workflow:** triage of new claims: category, priority and missing evidence.
- **Outcome measure:** median working days from lodgement to triage decision (case system).
- **Baseline:** 6 working days (April to June 2026).
- **Counter-metric:** share of triage decisions changed at assessment; baseline 11 %.
- **Decider:** Helen Park.

## 3. Data register

| Data | Subjects | Source | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Claim form and claim notes | claimants (Australian residents) | case management system (read) | injury and medical details, mental-health history, employment history, sometimes family violence disclosures |
| Medical reports attached to claims | claimants | case management system (read) | sensitive information |
| Officer decisions on past claims (2023-2026) | claimants | case system export | evaluation set |

## 4. Harms and cost of a wrong answer

- A wrong low priority delays payment to an injured person.
- A wrong category routes a claim to the wrong team and adds weeks.
- Officers may rely on the draft without reading the notes.
